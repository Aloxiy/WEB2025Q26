The Story of Galatea

Galatea takes its name from the ancient Greek myth of Pygmalion, a sculptor who created a beautiful ivory statue of a woman so perfect that he fell in love with his own creation. He prayed to the goddess Aphrodite to bring his sculpture to life. Touched by his devotion, Aphrodite granted his wish and transformed the statue into a living woman named Galatea, which means "she who is milk-white." Like Pygmalion breathing life into stone, our brand transforms artistic vision into living beauty through our creations.
